About Us

Who We Are: NMSA-UG is a non-profit student association for students from the Nuba Mountains studying in universities and colleges across Uganda.


Our Vision: A united, educated and empowered Nuba student community.


Our Mission: To support students academically, preserve Nuba culture, and build future leaders for our community.


Our Values: Unity, Respect, Education, Culture, Service


History: Founded to bridge the gap for Nuba students far from home, NMSA-UG offers community support and dynamic cultural ties. Our members predominantly pursue studies across leading regional institutions, including Kampala International University (KIU), Makerere University, and Kampala University.

Membership

Who Can Join: Any student from Nuba Mountains studying in Uganda, plus alumni and well-wishers.


Membership Benefits:

  • Academic support and mentorship
  • Help for new students arriving in Uganda
  • Cultural events and networking
  • Welfare support in difficult times
  • Leadership training

Membership Fees: Registration: UGX 10,000 | Annual: UGX 20,000


How to Join: 1. Fill the form, 2. Join our official WhatsApp Group, 3. Remit the required association fee.
